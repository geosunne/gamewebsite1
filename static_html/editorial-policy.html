Editorial Policy

BTW game organizes browser games with an emphasis on fast access, clear categories, and useful recommendations.

How games are organized

Games are grouped by category, tags, title, description, controls, and observed catalog patterns.

How recommendations are made

Catalog ordering uses available source votes, provider popularity, and legacy catalog play counts. These measures vary by provider and are not a directly comparable count of people playing on BTW game. Selection does not mean that every game has been fully play-tested.

What NEW means

NEW identifies games first added to the BTW game catalog within the last 14 days. A source release date, a metadata refresh, or a build does not reset that window.

What our checks mean

Source checks compare listing facts and instructions with the provider. Editorial review and player checks are separate records. A successful iframe response does not prove that the entire game, login, saving, or advertising works.

Accuracy standards

Game pages avoid invented ratings and avoid claiming specific mechanics unless they are present in the source data or game description.

FAQ

Why can a category change?

Categories can change when game data is cleaned or when a better fit is found for browsing and discovery.

Do you invent ratings?

No. If reliable rating data is not available, the site avoids adding fake aggregate ratings.
