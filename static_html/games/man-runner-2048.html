About Man Runner 2048

Man Runner 2048 is a casual arcade game where your objective is to run and merge men till you reach 2048. Your goal is to get the highest score on each round, because at the end of the level, you will get a chance to shoot a giant monster.

How to play Man Runner 2048

Your ultimate goal is to merge men to reach the 2048 level, achieve the highest score possible, and then fire powerful cannonballs to damage giant monsters and take control of the islands. Each course starts pretty simply. You will need to run through a course head-on, filled with obstacles, while collecting as many points as possible. The gamescape is set up like a runway, and obstacles are everywhere. They could be right in front of you, coming from below, dropping from above. Stay vigilant and keep your eye on the prize. You want to make it to the end of the course. Along the way, collect green rewards and pink diamonds for significant score boosts, but steer clear of anything red or items that split your points. At the end of the course, you will face a giant skeleton. Aim carefully to hit your target. Experiment to find its Achilles’ heel, sometimes right between the eyes, works for maximum damage to earn extra points. You may not be able to defeat it right away, but each attempt helps you rack up a higher score. Each round introduces new obstacles, including sliding floors that can help you gather more points if you move skillfully. Be careful of spikes that rise from the ground and bombs that drop from the sky, both of which can cost you the run. Between runs, you can buy accessories to customize your character.

Controls

Controls: Use the left mouse button or the AD or left-right arrow keys to move to the left and right. Use the left mouse button to aim the monster..
