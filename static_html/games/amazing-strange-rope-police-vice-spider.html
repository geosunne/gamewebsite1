How to play Amazing Strange Rope Police

You can choose to become a superhero and save the day, or you can be the bad guy and become a supervillain instead! The game is an open world, and you can interact with other characters and with items and climb buildings as you progress and understand the mechanics of Amazing Strange Rope Police. As your typical Spider-Man, you can launch a web, climb buildings and take down opponents with hero-like moves, but, as in GTA, you decide your fate and set your future, depending on whether you have chosen to be on the good or on the evil side. Same as in Grand Theft Auto, you can take any vehicle in the game and drive it, including helicopters. The 3D graphics in this game are immense, and the huge city you can explore is vast and full of surprises. You can use a myriad of superhero moves, such as a web shot, building climb, and laser beam, too! To top it all off, you can choose from various superhero costumes, too. Which path will you choose?

Controls

WASD or arrow keys: move T: open nearby garage Q: shoot web Right click: laser Left click: shoot or punch Space bar: jump Left shift: run X: switch weapons F: open car R: open nearby shop G: grenade Z: switch grenade C: switch vehicle camera Garbage truck controls Pitchfork up: Z Pitchfork down: X Dump start: Q Dump end: E Lift container: space.
