How to play Truck Simulator: Russia

Your objective is to transport cargo to and from different cities safely. Each job pays a certain amount, and collecting monetary rewards will help you upgrade your equipment for better driving. The more you earn, the more you can afford engine, brake, and steering upgrades. Choose a city to start from and go to, and get ready for the ultimate road trip. You can select one of many cities on the Russian map, from Saint Petersburg to Krasnoyarsk, which is more than 4.800 kms away from it and close to the border with Mongolia. This is not a suitable game for a quick play: the minimum time commitment to complete one of the first missions is 15 minutes. As a reward for investing this large amount of time in Truck Simulator: Russia, you get highly detailed graphics, especially if you can tweak the settings and bring the detail to the maximum level, and a lively, entertaining game which will make you feel like you are really crossing the border between Europe and Asia at the wheel of a big cargo truck. You can check your progress by clicking on stats, which will show you how much cargo you've delivered, how many trucks and trailers you've bought, how many car accidents you've had, and how much money you've earned. Once you have earned money, head to the garage for a smoother ride and take in the landscapes.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to drive Space bar to use handbrake C to change camera view.
