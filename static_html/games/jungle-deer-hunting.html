About Jungle Deer Hunting

Jungle Deer Hunting is a first-person shooter game that transports players to an African safari wildlife expedition, casting them within range of a variety of animals ready for the hunt. The objective is simple — shoot down a targeted number of animals throughout a series of landscapes, in some cases, using precision sniper skills.

How to play Jungle Deer Hunting

The game features a series of landscapes and animals to choose from, with unlocked levels that include: Club, Adventure, Dino, and Jeep Hunt. The game is not limited to deer hunting, and you’re not always in a jungle. Depending on the round, the shooter is tasked with hunting down lions, bears, deer, and more wild animals, even dinosaurs. All landscapes equip the shooter with an appropriate weapon, sometimes one of choice. Player’s progress will be tracked with a score card that tallies your rewards and how many animals you’ve hunted. There are also a series of locked levels: Bird Hunt and Pro Hunt. You can get to these levels once you’ve played a select number of levels of the unlocked landscapes.

Controls

Right-click = iron sight Left-click = shoot Scroll wheel = zoom in/out WASD / arrow keys = move Shift = sprint P = pause.

Tips for Jungle Deer Hunting

  • The key to the game is precision and patience. Some of the levels have targets on the animal’s back, particularly for those at a great distance. But don’t rely too much on time being on your side, the tables can turn, as animals might hunt you too.
