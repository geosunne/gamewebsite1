How to play Burnout Drift

The first car you start with is a Ford Mustang, but there are drifting classics like the Nissan 350z to pick-ups and supercars like the Bugatti Veyron. You can buy these powerhouse vehicles when you earn enough. You can use the money you earn from doing awesome drifts to buy new cars. Manage your speed and steering to keep your car drifting. Balancing acceleration and braking helps maintain longer drifts, which earn more points. There are three excellent tracks to zoom your latest set of wheels around in Burnout Drift. Rocky Pass is a typical mountain track with plenty of tricky turns and long corners — it’s a great middle-ground track. Ridge is a wide city road - probably the best track for drifting once you get into it. Winter Pass is a little tougher, featuring snow that slows you down and causes additional sliding.

Controls

Controls: W or up arrow to gas S or down arrow to brake AD or left/right arrow to steer Left shift/left Ctrl to gear up/down Space bar to handbrake F to use NOS G to enter slow motion I to start/stop the engine L to use low-beam headlights K to use high-beam headlights C to change the camera.

Tips for Burnout Drift

  • The easiest track to drift and earn money on is the Ridge The longer you keep up the same drift, the quicker you’ll earn points Crashing will cause you to lose all your drift points, so be careful! The cars all have unique characteristics, some better than others
