How to play Poxel.io

Invite your friends to play with you by hosting a game with them and playing in real-time together. Play the game more to continue progressing and advancing your levels so you can earn PX coins and buy skins, characters, hats, back items, and more! Keep playing the game, and you can eventually reach the top of the leaderboard, so you can show the world that you are #1 in Poxel.io Poxel.io is fast-paced multiplayer chaos where sharp reflexes and smart movement are your keys to survival. You’ll need eyes in the back of your head to stay alive and to climb your way to the top of the leaderboard. You’re dropped into a maze-like battleground armed and ready. Your goal? Take down enemies while dodging incoming fire. But don’t just shoot anything that moves, some players are your teammates. They’ll be clearly labeled, so watch your fire. What goes around definitely comes around. You can host private games and invite friends to battle in real time. The more you play, the more you progress, the more you earn PX coins and gems, which you can use to unlock cool content.

Controls

Controls: WASD = move Space = jump X or Q = dash Enter = chat P or Esc = pause Left-click = shoot Right-click or E = iron sight.

Tips for Poxel.io

  • Stick to the walls: Keep your back covered to avoid sneak attacks. Take the high ground: Rooftops offer a better view and sniping opportunities. Stay alert: Enemies can come from any direction, so keep moving and scanning your surroundings.
