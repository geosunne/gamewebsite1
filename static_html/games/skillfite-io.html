How to play Skillfite.io

Start from scratch Skillfite has many features reminiscent of old-school browser-based MMORPGs like Runescape. Starting as a level 1 noob with no gear or experience, you have to chop trees, mine rocks, and catch fish to level up your skills and sustain yourself during battles with low-level enemies like rabbits. Harvest and craft Use the raw materials you gain from chopping and mining to craft armor for protection and weapons for attack. Early in the game, you can craft gear from the wood you’ve earned, but you can quickly find rocks to mine copper and craft something slightly better. There are 15 resource types in Skillfite.io. Build your skills Level up your skills to reach milestones that unlock better resources. For example, when you reach level 5 wood chopping skills, you can cut down the more giant pinewood trees. It’s worth attacking animals, even peaceful ones, as they drop loot, which can be helpful. Explore various zones There are various zones to explore in Skillfite.io. Teleport around the map and head into non-PvP, PvP, and high-risk PvP areas. In the high-risk PvP areas, you can team up with and fight players of any level. You can initiate trades with players in all zones.
