How to play Waffle Words

Waffle Words is an addictive word game where you have to create words from a waffle grid of letters. The words can be created as long as the line you draw is not interrupted. Lines can be horizontal, vertical, L-shaped, U-shaped - you name it! Do not limit yourself to one straight, left-to-right line/letter combination. Note that you can't create diagonal ones. Your words must be at least three characters in length, so avoid relying on two-letter words to get through the game. Time Limit There is a two-minute time limit to find as many words as possible. Can you see the longest possible word? How about the word with the highest score in this short time frame? Six Languages Waffle Words can be played in six different languages. The words are available in the following languages: English German Spanish French Italian Portuguese
