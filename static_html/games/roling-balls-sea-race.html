How to play Rolling Balls Sea Race

In Rolling Balls Sea Race, you control a donut-shaped rolling ball, and while it sounds easy, it’s all about precision and balance. You can roll forward, backward, and side to side, but don’t get too comfortable – one wrong move and you’ll be splashing into the sea, losing valuable time! The challenge ramps up quickly as you race through tight curves and tricky obstacles. The real trick is finding the perfect mix of speed and control. As you navigate the track, you'll need to dodge obstacles while collecting coins scattered along the way. The more coins you grab, the more rewards you unlock, including new rolling objects that are easier to control and give you a competitive edge. For extra fun, jump into the 2-player mode and compete head-to-head with a friend! The race gets even wilder with more laughs and some serious competition. Keep your balance, avoid the falls, and master the curves to be the first to reach the finish line!
