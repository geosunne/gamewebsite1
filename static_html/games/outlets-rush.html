How to play Outlets Rush

Are you ready to run a high-speed retail empire? Outlets Rush puts you center stage as a manager of a shopping mall. Your task is to build each shop, starting with Neki, a sports shop, from the bottom up. Start with a sneaker shop, stocking shelves straight from the warehouse as shipments roll in. Keep up with demand by quickly restocking and rushing to the counter to check out eager customers because the line isn’t getting any shorter! As you rack up earnings, reinvest in your store. Expand with new shelves for sneakers and T-shirts, unlock skateboards for faster movement, and balance stocking with customer service to maximize profits. Want to work smarter, not harder? Open an HR office to hire staff or upgrade your speed and capacity to keep the store running smoothly. With the right strategy, you’ll be filling shelves faster, making more money, and scaling up operations. Once your shop is thriving, expand your business! Earn enough stars to unlock new stores and become a retail tycoon. But be careful: each new shop requires careful management to run independently before you move on.
