About Autogun Heroes

Autogun Heroes is an action arcade platformer game that throws you into the heart of an alien-infested battleground alongside a team of fearless heroes. Armed with overloaded weapons and the ability to stack endless upgrades, you’ll blast through relentless waves of enemies in a vivid, fast-paced world teeming with chaos.

How to play Autogun Heroes

Autogun Heroes is a vibrant, action-packed shooter where the chaos never stops, and your eye-patched, muscle-bound hero, Gus, is on a mission of vengeance. The game features automatic shooting, so your focus is all about movement and aim—use the WASD keys to navigate, the space bar to jump, and press down to drop. As you blast through enemies, you’ll travel across dynamic levels filled with surprises, enemies attacking from all directions, and plenty of opportunities to prove your skills. At the edge of each area, you’ll be transported to new sections of the battlefield, keeping the action fresh and intense. Along the way, you’ll unlock a cast of epic characters, each with their own unique flair and abilities, like Odessa, the masked undercover spy, Doc Justice, the gunslinging cowboy, and Spark, a plasma-wielding inventor. To access these heroes, you’ll need game credits, which you can earn as you progress. Power up your gameplay with easy-to-earn upgrades like bullet power, increased hit points, or veteran status. Advanced skills are also available through Alien DNA upgrades, giving you access to a robust skill tree to enhance your strategy. Throughout the game, you’ll encounter thrilling perks like poison attacks, bullet wall bounces, and even the rare chance of a headshot for instant kills. Watch ads to unlock certain stages or cool off between battles. Equip items at basecamp, upgrade your gear between levels, and take the fight to the Galactic Alliance worlds to free them from the clutches of the Vortex Invaders. With enemies swarming from above, below, and all around, quick reflexes and sharp aim are your keys to survival in this non-stop, adrenaline-filled adventure.
