How to play Subway Clash Remastered

Explore the map and shoot the enemy team. Pick up weapons along the way. There are various weapons you’ll find, from the default AK-47 to a grenade launcher, rocket launcher, and shotgun. Rack up enough points to defeat your enemy by killing as many as you can, then restart the game!

Controls

LMB - shoot RMB - aim WASD or arrow keys - movement C - crouch SPACE - jump TAB - score table.
