How to play Color Tap: Coloring by Numbers

Use the left mouse button to choose and apply colours. Use the mouse wheel to zoom when a small region is difficult to see.

Controls

Controls: Use the left mouse button to select and apply the color Use the mouse wheel to zoom in/out the pallet.

Tips for Color Tap: Coloring by Numbers

  • Zoom in to finish small regions before changing the view.
  • Work through one colour at a time when the picture contains many similar areas.
