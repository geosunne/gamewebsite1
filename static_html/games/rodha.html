How to play Rodha

Your job in Rodha is seemingly simple. You are meant to get through each level without getting taken out. You'll be leaping over walls, spikes, and gaps, all while trying to make it to the portal at the end. Watch out for thin platforms as they vanish shortly after you land on them, so keep moving and don't linger. Checkpoints come in the form of flags scattered through each level. Run past one, and it's yours, so if you fall, you'll pick back up from there rather than starting all over. Rodha is a fast-paced game, so if you get distracted, even for a second, you will find yourself stopped in your tracks and back to the beginning. Some levels have stars hidden in them. Grab those whenever you can, as they're your ticket to unlocking new skins back in the main menu. Coins are also worth collecting throughout your run, and you can spend them in the shop on 9 unique character skins ranging from 50 to 250 coins. Rodha has a bit of a learning curve at first. You need to get used to the controls, but more importantly, to the speed. Once the timing of your jumps starts to click, the whole game opens up, so stay patient, and you will find yourself moving on from level to level in no time.

Controls

A / left arrow key = jump to the left D / right arrow key = jump to the right Press the button twice for a double jump.
