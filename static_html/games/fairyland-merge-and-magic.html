How to play Fairyland Merge & Magic

Embark on a merging adventure Start your Fairland merge adventure on the sunny Fairyland shores of this pristine fantasy world. Your journey starts with learning how to use your merge magic to create delicious fruits and treats to sell to the fairies. From there, you’ll go on a series of exciting quests with your Fairy Godmother and magical aid guiding you at every step. Harness your magical energy You’ll be able to clear the clouds and open more areas using keys to progress through this enchanted kingdom. Your dreams will soon become reality as you merge dragons, unicorns, leprechauns, and other mythical creatures to populate your fantasy world. Collect rewards for your progress There are many ways to earn rewards in Fairyland Merge & Magic! You can collect coins for every new item and level you unlock, pop open chests filled with enchanted goodies, and unlock bonus areas full of magical surprises.
