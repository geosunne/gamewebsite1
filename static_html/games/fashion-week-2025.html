How to play Fashion Week 2025

Step into the spotlight with Fashion Week 2025, where each round brings a new style challenge. Start by choosing from five unique fashion themes: glam, casual, office chic, weekend cool, and a night out on the town. Begin with a full glam makeover: pick a stunning hairstyle like a bun or body wave, match it with a stylish outfit, and finish the look with shoes and accessories. When your heart barometer fills up, you're ready for the next challenge. Next, dress down for a cozy movie night. Go for a sporty top, like a jersey or T-shirt, and pair it with joggers or sweats. Add colorful sneakers and a hint of jewelry to complete the look. For the office, go sleek and elegant with a neat ponytail, loafers or heels, and a polished outfit. A pop of color from your handbag will tie it all together. Weekend brunch calls for relaxed, athletic vibes. Choose a fresh tee or sweatshirt, match it with shorts, leggings, or a tennis skirt, and accessorize with sneakers, sunglasses, and earrings. Wrap up with a bold day-out ensemble. Try frilled or lace tops, jeans or leather pants, and top it off with a chic handbag and accessories. Take photos of your completed looks, save them in a group shot, and share your fashion creations with friends. Then jump back in and style again!
