How to play Geometry Game

Press Space or the Up arrow to jump, and hold to repeat jumps where appropriate. Vehicle sections use the arrow keys to steer up or down.

Controls

Controls: SPACE/UP Arrow = Jump HOLD DOWN SPACE/UP Arrow = Multi-jump UP Arrow = Steer vehicle up DOWN Arrow = Steer vehicle down Don't touch the spikes, or you will have to restart!.

Tips for Geometry Game

  • Learn the position of the next obstacle rather than relying on rhythm alone.
  • Check for a change in movement controls when a new section begins.
