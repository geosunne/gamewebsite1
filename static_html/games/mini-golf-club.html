About Mini Golf Club

Mini Golf Club is a multiplayer mini-golf game where you compete against real players across short, themed courses. Your objective is to sink the ball in as few strokes as possible before the timer runs out.

How to play Mini Golf Club

You have a few choices to make when you start the game: Play Online: Jump into a multiplayer match against real players. Daily Hole: A special hole that resets every day, with a countdown timer showing when the next one arrives. Career: Work your way through 612 holes of solo play. Create: Build your own hole. Join: Enter a match or course someone else has set up. Best: Browse top-rated community creations. You'll also find the Shop and Settings buttons in the bottom corner, plus a Discord link if you want to find the community.

Controls

Controls: Drag the left mouse button = shoot ball C = camera mode Arrow keys / Mouse = look around Page up / mouse wheel up = zoom in Page down / mouse wheel down = zoom out.
