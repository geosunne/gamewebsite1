How to play Bridge Race

Bridge Race is an oddly satisfying game where you aim to collect as many blocks of your color as possible and carry them across the board to build a bridge. The objective is to create a path that leads you to the next collection area and, ultimately, to the finish line. Victory is sweet, but the challenge continues as each round gets more difficult. The game is designed to be both simple and rewarding, with delicate sounds that create a satisfying atmosphere. You'll encounter obstacles like slides and elevators, which add a fun element as you navigate through the level, taking you closer to the next challenge. But you’re not alone in this race. Other players are also collecting blocks to build their own bridges, and they’re more than happy to challenge you along the way. If you’re not careful, your opponents can sabotage your progress by building over your bridge, undoing all the hard work you’ve done. And if you run out of blocks and need to return to collect more, be prepared for your opponents to steal your blocks, changing their color to match yours and forcing you to start the process over again. You’ll also have to watch out for being knocked down by other players, which causes you to lose all the blocks on your back. Throughout the game, you’ll encounter rewards scattered across the map. Green rewards multiply the number of blocks you carry, giving you an advantage, while red ones will diminish your collection, so it’s important to avoid them at all costs. Stay vigilant, collect wisely, and you’ll be on your way to victory! The game is full of obstacles to watch out for, and gates scattered around each floor can make or break your race. Green gates boost your points, either multiplying what you're carrying by x2 or adding a flat bonus like +9, while red gates subtract blocks, with penalties like -5, so don’t run through those! The multiplier gates are where strategy comes in - the more blocks you have on your back, the more it makes sense to walk through multipliers. So for example, if you have three blocks on your back and you walk through a x2 multiplier, it doesn’t really pay off as much as it would with more blocks to double. It’s also a good idea to look below you. Some floors have open holes cut right into the middle of the board, and falling through one drops you off the platform entirely, so steer around them even if the shortest route to a block cluster runs straight across. The safest route is rarely the straightest one. Every level is a race to the podium, and finishing last means failing the level outright. When that happens, you can restart and try again or watch a short ad to skip ahead to the next level. Finish first and you're crowned on the top spot of the podium with a Victory banner and three stars, with your closest rivals filling out second and third place below you.
