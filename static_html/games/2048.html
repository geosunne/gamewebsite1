About 2048

2048 is a single-player sliding tile puzzle game developed by Gabriele Cirulli. Merge tiles with the same power of 2 to get the total sum.

How to play 2048

The game begins on a simple 4x4 grid, with two numbered tiles appearing at random spots. Each tile is either a 2 or a 4. You will need to slide these tiles around the board and merge matching numbers until you eventually create the ultimate 2048 tile. It's not easy at first, but once you discover the patterns and better understand the rules of the game, you will find a solid rhythm you can keep going back to. Moves are easy to make. All you need to do is swipe or press the arrow keys to slide the tiles in one direction or another. Note that every move you make shifts the entire board; this means that all tiles travel until they hit either the wall or another tile. If two tiles of the same number collide, they merge into a single tile with double the value. For example, a 2 and a 2 combine to form a 4, two 4s become an 8, and so on. After each move, a new tile, again a 2 or a 4, appears on the board, slowly filling up the empty spaces. Use your arrow keys to move all the tiles in the specified direction. Note that every time you move, another two are spawned into the game. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one, doubling the value. Consider your movements and avoid sliding tiles around haphazardly. Strategy is key in this game. The board keeps filling up each time you move! If the board fills all the way without you reaching 2048, it's game over. The challenge is pretty much keeping the board from filling up completely. If there are no more moves left (no empty spaces and no adjacent matches), the game is over. The further you go, the more challenging it becomes to manage the board, as high-value tiles start to crowd out smaller numbers you still need for merging. Keep your higher-value tiles tucked away in one corner, build around them, and resist the temptation of false merges. Think a few steps ahead: merging a pair might look good now, but it could also block future moves.

Tips for 2048

  • Focus on the edge of the grid. You want the highest number in a lovely corner for easy access. Keep making the larger numbers around that corner so you can combine them when they match. If you end up with the higher numbers towards the middle, you can quickly get lost with inaccessible numbers, and eventually, you will have to start a new game.
