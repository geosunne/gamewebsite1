How to play Steve's World

Use the arrow keys to move Steve, and press Z to jump. You can jump over various enemies and jump on top of their heads to kill them like in Mario and other classic platformer games.

Controls

Controls: Profile 1 Arrow keys = move Z = jump X = shoot fireball (when available) Profile 2 WASD = move K = jump J = shoot fireball (when available) Mobile Use the touch buttons.

Tips for Steve's World

  • Bump your head into the star blocks to get bonus items like coins, lives, a cape, and fireballs (fire using X). The cape allows you to reach higher coins and bonuses. Dying in Steve’s World returns you to the start of the level, so be cautious.
