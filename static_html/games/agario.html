How to play Agar.io

In Agar.io, you begin as a tiny cell in a massive arena filled with pellets and other players. At the start, your best move is to quietly gather pellets scattered around the map. This lets you grow without drawing too much attention from larger predators. Avoid chasing other small players right away—most beginners get eaten this way. Patience pays off: focus on steady growth until you’re big enough to hunt. The game has 2 simple rules to follow: You can only consume targets that are smaller than you to evolve Larger agar will eat you Slide around the Agario battleground, trying to exist as a single cell looking to evolve. Think like a survivor - don’t just avoid bigger agar, go in for the kill and eat as much as you can! Pick up all the smaller blobs and eat the other players to grow larger in size. Avoid blobs bigger than you as they will consume you, which means game over! But you can always start the game over and go for your next best record. Once you reach medium size, movement slows, so you’ll need to be more tactical. Use your ability to split (Space key or split button) to lunge at smaller cells just out of reach. A successful split doubles your number of cells but halves their size, so choose your targets wisely. Always make sure the prey is small enough that even your split cell can absorb them; otherwise, you’ll just leave yourself vulnerable. To climb higher, learn to use viruses to your advantage. As a small cell, viruses act as safe zones where big predators won’t dare follow. As a larger cell, you can eject mass into a virus to fire it toward enemies, popping them into pieces and giving you a chance to swallow their fragments. This is one of the most effective ways to topple giants on the leaderboard. Winning in Agar.io isn’t about brute force; it’s about survival, timing, and positioning. Hug the edges of the map when you’re small, move toward the center once you’re stronger, and always look for opportunities to trap opponents against the map’s borders. The ultimate goal is to balance risk and reward: grow big enough to dominate, but not so reckless that a single virus or split leaves you scattered and eaten.
