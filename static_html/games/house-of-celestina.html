About House of Celestina

House of Celestina is a 3D survival horror game that can keep you on the edge of your seat, but only for so long, as the game's objective is to find the key to escape. The game is designed to explore the House of Celestina, where you'll find clues and keys to help you figure out where you are and how to get out.

How to play House of Celestina

You have been kidnapped and find yourself in the house of the evil Celestina. Why are you there, and what are her plans? There is no time to think it through, or she is likely to sneak up on you. You must move around the house, scouring every dark corridor, room, and corner, all in the dark, with only the narrow light from a flashlight to guide you. Each level is a different puzzle to solve. You will receive clues along the way, sometimes from past victims of Celestina who didn’t make it out, to help you find what you are ultimately looking for - the keys to escape this house of ghouls. Be wary, as Celestina and her spooky companions will pop up when you least expect them, and when she does, your blood pressure will go through the roof! To lower your blood pressure, you need to press the space bar like your life depends on it because it does. If you don’t lower your blood pressure on time, it’s game over for you. Can you survive this creepy game and escape with your life?
