About The Prank King

The Prank King is a laugh-out-loud casual game where you step into the shoes of prankster Nick, the ultimate trickster on a mission to outwit Misty, the notoriously cranky Scary Teacher. Each level is a new adventure as you sneak around her house, setting up hilarious traps and creating chaos at every turn.

How to play The Prank King

She's snoozing away, blissfully unaware of the chaos that's about to unfold. But you're not here to let her sleep, are you? Time to give her the rudest wake-up call ever! You have two deliciously devious options: slather her alarm clock with sticky glue or blast her awake with an ear-splitting air horn. Which one will jolt her out of dreamland? Yep, you know it—the air horn! As the deafening noise fills the room, she shoots out of bed, already regretting her decision to close her eyes. But the fun is just beginning! Now, she needs to brush her teeth. What could make this simple task a morning nightmare? Should you sneakily replace her toothpaste with fiery hot sauce or maybe swap it with a dollop of shaving cream? If you chose hot sauce, you’re on a roll! Watch her eyes widen in shock as the fiery paste hits her tongue—she’s not just awake now. She’s wide-eyed and fuming. In a desperate attempt to cool off, she grabs her trusty thermos for a refreshing drink. But you're not about to let her off easy! Should you spike it with a splash of vinegar or go soft with some soda? Soda? Nah, that’s way too nice! Vinegar is the ultimate prankster's choice. As she takes a big swig, her face twists in disgust—now she’s really boiling, and you’ve just leveled up to round two! It's your choice The Prank King is all about making mischievous choices that turn everyday moments into a comedy of errors. From swapping her shampoo with bleach and setting up sneaky tripwires to tying her shoes together before she hits the treadmill and sabotaging her barbells with glue, you’re the mastermind behind every misstep, every tumble, and every furious outburst. Your mission? To keep Scary Teacher stumbling, falling, and absolutely losing her mind at every turn. It’s the ultimate game of pranks where the goal is simple: make her day as miserable—and hilarious—as possible!
