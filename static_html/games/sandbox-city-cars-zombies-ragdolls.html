About Sandbox City

Sandbox City is a simulation horror sandbox game where you roam a city taken over by zombies. You can drive cars, fight the enemy off with guns, complete missions for cash, and more, all while you try to avoid hungry zombies.

How to play Sandbox City

The game starts with your character and accessories ready to be customized. This is your home base before getting into the gameplay. Start with customizing your character or your car using the two Customize buttons, or open Missions, the Shop, or Settings from the top right corner. It's also worth reviewing the full controls list on this screen, which has tabs for Keyboard, Xbox Controller, and PS Controller. The game also supports Xbox and PlayStation controllers. When you're ready, hit Play to enter the city. The first time you press Play, the Mission Guy walks you through a quick intro. He welcomes you to Sandbox City, warns you that zombies are on the loose and the citizens are in danger, points out the Shop where you can arm yourself, and tells you to come find him for missions. Follow the on-screen marker (it shows the distance, like "Talk to Mission Guy 114 m") to reach him and start earning.

Controls

Controls: WASD or arrow keys = move Left-click = attack. Hold to shoot Hold shift = run Space = jump when on foot, handbrake when in the car E = enter/exit car, buy items R = switch between weapons T = enter and exit Slo-Mo mode L = lock / unlock mouse cursor C = change camera perspective Esc = quit.

Tips for Sandbox City

  • Claim your freebies daily. The free Daily Ammo, the Daily Reward, and the daily missions add up fast. Upgrade your weapons. The Baseball Bat and Tennis Racket only cost 50 cash, and the melee daily mission actually pays more than the gun one.
