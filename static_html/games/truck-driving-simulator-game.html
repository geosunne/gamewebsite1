How to play Truck Driving Simulator Game

Navigate through exciting terrains while transporting a variety of cargo, including livestock, weapons, and cars. With customizable trucks and multiple missions, embark on a journey through rural landscapes, mastering the art of trucking in this immersive simulator. WASD keys or arrow keys control movement. As you move backward, the game's perspective shifts in tandem with your movement, providing a full view of the landscape behind you. Begin by choosing your game mode. The game offers several modes, which include: Animal Transport Mode Robbery Mode Parking Mode Open World Oil Transport Euro Truck Travel in India Police Truck Chase Once you've chosen your mode, you can select your truck and get ready to enjoy the landscape. Each mode offers a different selection, but the backdrop of the game is that it is set in Asia, with peculiar references to the countries of India and Pakistan, especially. Every mode presents different tasks and challenges, such as attaching and transporting oil tankers, capturing and transporting livestock, driving tractors back to the farm, and avoiding collisions with cattle while driving in India, among others. As usual with driving games, completing missions allows you to earn in-game currency, which in turn is helpful to buy new vehicles and improve your ride. A unique, unlockable feature of this game is to provide users with the ability to write poetry on the truck lorry, a common practice in South East Asia, where "Truck Art" is a popular phenomenon.

Controls

W/upper arrow = forward movement A/left arrow = left movement D/right arrow = right movement S/back arrow = backward movement/reverse Space = brake P = game pause H = horn.
