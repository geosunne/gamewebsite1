About Space Wars Battleground

Space Wars Battleground is an action battle game set in a faraway galaxy where battles are fast, numerous, and to the death. The game features numerous characters that you play with and against people from around the world to dominate the competitive leaderboard and reach the heights of galactic victory with the support of your fellow fighters.

How to play Space Wars Battleground

At the beginning of each match, you start with zero experience points, putting everyone on an equal footing. To gain experience, you must deal with damage and take down enemy team characters, steadily building your strength as the battle progresses. Once you've accumulated enough experience, you can spend it on purchasing new characters, each with their own unique abilities and playstyles, adding depth and strategy to your gameplay. After each match, you'll also earn coins as rewards for your performance. These coins can be used to unlock a variety of weapons, character skins, and upgrades, allowing you to customize and enhance your arsenal. The ultimate goal is to work with your team to eliminate 100 enemy characters first, securing victory and bragging rights in this intense, action-packed competition. How to find your enemy It's key to use the floor plan map in the top corner of the game, which shows you exactly where your opponents are hiding and, more importantly, where your allies are as well. You want to be strategic about where your support is needed and avoid shooting friendly fire. Coordinate your moves based on where you won't be ambushed, where you can help your team, and where you have the best chance to shoot your opponents.
