How to play Mafia Takedown

The goal of Mafia Takedown is to avoid being murdered by the many staff, lovers, family, friends, police, or rigged falling objects! As the mafia Don, you'll enjoy extreme protection, but it also means you'll have to look over your shoulder constantly. The game begins with you as one of the Don's foes, with one clear intention: to literally stab the Don in the back, strip him of his clothes, and gain immediate mafia power and protection. Once you are the Don, you will strut all the way to the end of the corridor to meet your final fate. Will that be dinner with your lover or a lifetime in prison? It all depends on how savvy you are and how quickly you can spot your friends from your foes. The cycle continues from there. It's kill or be killed, so keep your eyes wide open. The gameplay is relatively straightforward. A series of people will approach you, tiptoeing toward your back. Once you see them raise their arms to either strike, stab, or shoot you, you must immediately turn and catch them in the act, calling your bodyguards to send them to a lifetime of prison life quickly. But be warned, not everyone is out to get you. Some of the people approaching you may very well want to kiss you! If you're too cautious, you may find yourself with a life of no love. Unlock Scenarios Various scenarios can be unlocked, each leading to a different end. You may be swarmed with love at a romantic dinner, poisoned by your butler, captured by your chef, turned into a chihuahua... yes, anything is possible! The fun part of this game is unlocking the scenarios, so don't be overly cautious if you just want to have fun and explore the difference between friends and foes. Be careful of the stampeding parade and police officers. Once they catch you, it's game over!
