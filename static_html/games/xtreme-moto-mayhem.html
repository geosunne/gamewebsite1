How to play Xtreme Moto Mayhem

Your mission in Xtreme Moto Mayhem is to gain as many reward points as possible while making your way to the next level via motorcycle stunts, flips, and challenges. Each level features a series of obstacle course racing paths, complete with near-impossible ramps and more. If you're a fan of (digital) extreme sports, this is the game for you. Rev up your engines and hit the gas with the W key, but don’t think you can coast through these thrilling tracks on autopilot! Each round presents a unique set of exhilarating challenges that will keep you on your toes. From treacherous, collapsing floors that might give way beneath you to daring half ramps soaring high in the sky, every twist and turn demands your full attention. Brace yourself for sliding doors that can unexpectedly alter your path and elevator shafts that add a vertical twist to the action. And that’s just the beginning: each level is packed with creative obstacles that will test your reflexes and driving skills. The key to the game is to become familiar with the control keys and the various moves you can make. Once you have this down, you can have fun and test your reflexes. This is where you get to practice your stunts, including forward and backward flips. These may not be reserved for vanity purposes. Some of these flips are ideal ways to overcome a few obstacles.

Controls

Controls: W / Up Arrow Key = Acceleration A / Left Arrow Key = Lean Back (To perform backflips) D / Right Arrow Key = Lean Front (To perform front flips) S / Down Arrow Key = Brake Space Bar = Jump P = Pause.

Tips for Xtreme Moto Mayhem

  • Make the brake key (S) your friend throughout the game; it will get you out of sticky situations, but don't brake too fast; you may find yourself flat on the floor Maximize air time because more of it will earn you more coins at the end of the race
