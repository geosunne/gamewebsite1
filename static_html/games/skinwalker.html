How to play Skinwalker

In Skinwalker, players find themselves alone in a chilling forest where their primary objective is to keep a fire burning to survive the cold while evading a terrifying creature known as the Skinwalker. To play effectively, you'll need to gather resources, such as sticks, to maintain the fire's warmth. As you explore the environment, beware of the dangerous Skinwalker, which disguises itself among the wildlife. You’ll encounter various items and tools, including a shovel, but it's crucial to understand how to use them strategically. You'll need to search for wooden planks to cross bridges and to locate the key to a safe. The game requires quick thinking, as you’ll have limited time before the cold sets in, and if you stray too far from your fire, you risk freezing to death. As you navigate through the forest, always be on high alert; the Skinwalker could be lurking around any corner. Utilize your gathered materials wisely and keep the fire alive to outlast the dangers that await you. The key to survival lies in managing your time and resources effectively while confronting the chilling threat that haunts the woods. This is not for the faint of heart, so tread lightly and keep that fire burning!
