You get to choose quirky channel themes like hilarious meme breakdowns or classic heartwarming cat videos and upgrade your gear to produce top-notch content, even better cat videos.

How to play Idle Vlogger Simulator

You start your blogging journey in Idle Blogger Simulator by customizing your channel with a catchy name (or not so catchy) and unique avatar. Once you have these basics down, it's time to create. The objective is to create as many videos as possible. You are given themes for your videos—whether it’s gaming, lifestyle, the latest trends, or cats—and are given prompts to decide how what to focus on most in your video. You get to adjust the type of content you want to emphasize but be warned; your base followers will grow depending on whether you find that perfect sweet spot. Each theme has specific requirements, so think strategically to make your content shine. Once your video is published, it will start collecting views, but the key to success is consistency—the more videos you create, the faster your fanbase and income grow. Invest in upgrades to boost views and subscribers as your channel gains popularity. Unlock new features like advanced video equipment, fresh locations to blog from, and even additional avatars to represent your growing brand. Pay attention to trends, as trending topics generate the most buzz and help you attract hordes of new followers. You’ll get prompts like "a famous blogger giving your channel a shoutout or a brand offering a sponsorship deal after you mention their product." These opportunities can significantly boost your views and earnings, which will help you grow and eventually help you hire a crew, including scriptwriters, managers, and PR experts, to help take your channel to the next level. Other prompts include: "In one of your recent videos, you mentioned a headphone that you use yourself. You were contacted by a representative of the company and offered money." Take the money and keep the amazing conversation with your viewers going! Keep the momentum going by creating and publishing videos regularly. With each step, you’ll unlock new tools, rewards, and challenges that make the journey to internet stardom become more of a reality. Don't forget your friends when you make it to the top. Tip: Follow the trends because trending videos are the ones that get the most subscribers!
