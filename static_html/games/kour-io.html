How to play Kour.io

In Kour.io, your main goal is to earn KP (Kour Points) by racking up kills and completing missions. The more KP you collect, the more you can unlock! Spend your KP on various customizations like skins, crates, hats, characters, and emotes. You'll level up as you gather points in games, gaining access to even more content and perks. Want to team up with friends? Create or join clans to form a powerful squad and take on challenges together. The competition is fierce, so aim for the top spots on the daily, monthly, and all-time leaderboards to prove your dominance in the Kour.io universe. Whether you’re playing solo or with your clan, every match is a chance to climb the ranks and earn bragging rights. For a personal touch, dive into the map editor to create your own custom maps and challenge other players in your unique arenas. You can also explore the server finder to discover and join lobbies across Kour.io, offering endless opportunities for adventure and competition. Whether you’re customizing your experience or conquering leaderboards, Kour.io offers a dynamic and engaging world to master! Kour.io Characters You can choose from nine unlocked players, each equipped with a different weapon. Players and weapons include: Soldier: AK-47 Gun, 100 HP Hitman: Sniper Rifle, 90 HP Gunner: P90 Gun, 90 HP Heavy: Machine Gun, 150 HP Rocketeer: Rocket Launcher, 90 HP Agent: Silenced MP5, 100 HP Brawler: Shotgun, 120 HP Assassin: FAMAS, 100 HP Investor: Scar Rifle, 90 HP (free unlock with an ad) If you get 10+ kills, you'll unlock even more characters. Juggernaut: Minigun, 100 HP Recon: Vector, 80 HP Pyro: Flamethrower, 100 HP Rayblader: Laser Gun, 110 HP Have a go at each player, as they all possess different qualities and various weapons. Once you've chosen your player, you can get down to business. Choose Your Map Havana: Based on the Cuban city, it's the first and most competitive map, set in a tropical paradise. Ghost Town: Inspired by Nuke Town from Call of Duty, this map features an abandoned desert town in Arizona with rooftop warfare. Snowstorm: Set in Iceland, this winter map lets you engage enemies amid freezing ocean views and purple skies. Kour House: The newest map with boost pads, shipment containers, and buildings for strategic fights. Legion HQ: Modeled after the Legion Platforms headquarters, this office space map is perfect for fun trick shots. Game Modes Official game modes: Free For All Team Deathmatch Gun Game, only played with guns Hardpoint, played in teams, points are accrued for killing enemies and for touching a base on the map ParKour, where shooting happens while parkouring between buildings MoonSnipe, a low-gravity mode played on Earth’s most ancient satellite KourSurf, where surf and shooting happen simultaneously Kour io has a vibrant community of players, and they are also creating game modes. Among those available, there is: KourCraft, inspired by Minecraft and Fortnite, allows you to build structures to help you fight FFA 35, a 35-minute Free For All Pool Map
