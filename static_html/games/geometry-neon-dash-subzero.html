How to play Geometry Dash Subzero

Geometry Neon Dash Subzero is not for the faint of heart. It is a high-speed action platformer game that requires timing, stealth, and memory. Guide your cube through levels upon levels, donned in dazzling neon lights while maintaining a constant jumping motion to avoid obstacles below, above, and even straight on. Be sure to time your jumps perfectly, or you will have to start from the beginning. As you progress, you must also try to collect white orbs that you can use to purchase new cube models from the store. Your points will be tallied at the end of each level, measuring your time, the number of jumps taken, and the number of level attempts. All factors into your points.

Tips for Geometry Dash Subzero

  • Be prepared for anything. Your cube will enter spheres that allow you to fly, and certain bounces will take you backwards. Keep your eye on the very next step and focus on being reactive. That’s how you will get ahead.
