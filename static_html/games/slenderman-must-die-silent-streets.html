About Slenderman Must Die: Silent Streets

Slenderman Must Die: Silent Streets is an intense and chilling first person shooter game with horror elements. You are trapped within a small village and you must search for the legendary Slenderman who haunts the streets – this iconic figure has plagued people throughout history and it is up to you to find him and eliminate him once and for all!
