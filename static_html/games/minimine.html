How to play Mini Mine

Your mining adventure starts with an honorable task: freeing a kidnapped noob girl trapped by Cheater and claiming the coveted god’s pickaxe. Your mission includes exploring new islands, building bridges, and uncovering unique tools to assist you in your journey. Resources are key, and they can be gathered by delving into the mine. Your primary goal is to mine gems and resources of various types, including orange, green, blue, and white gems, coins, stones, and more. Start mining by walking to the main block and chipping away continuously. Extracted rewards will scatter: simply walk over them to collect them. Keep an eye out for alerts to “jump into the portal.” Entering the portal will occasionally replenish your mine, revealing new, unique, and more valuable resources to collect. However, note that the mine won't always replenish when you jump into the portal. Mining has a gradual pace and requires persistence. The more you mine, the more rewards and currency you accumulate, unlocking the ability to purchase better tools and progress to higher levels. Early levels may feel slow: for instance, by level 2, you might have only mined seven coins. But patience is essential, as higher rewards and tools become accessible as you advance. Tools & Upgrades Use your earnings to purchase tools and potions that enhance your mining efficiency: Potions start at $2500 and include: Double block destruction speed. Double the number of mined items. Increase resource production from quarries and sawmills. Pickaxe cost Stone Pickaxe: $100 Iron Pickaxe: $500 Golden Pickaxe: $2000 Diamond Pickaxe: $5000 Cheater Pickaxe: $15,000 Game Tips Sell your stones. This will allow you to boost your purchase power by selling stones at the store. Slow and steady wins the race. Prices are high, so consistent mining is crucial to afford the best tools and upgrades. Invest in potions and upgraded pickaxes as soon as possible to speed up your progress.
