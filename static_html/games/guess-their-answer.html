How to play Guess Their Answer

Guess Their Answer is a guessing game similar to Family Feud, where you try to predict the most popular answers given to specific questions. You start by giving your character a name and meet your opponent. You’re presented with a question or prompt with an audience watching you. You have to type your answer, and when you get the right answer, the audience will rally by your side. The more audience you have on your side, the more you know you’re winning. You will be told when your opponent gets wrong answers, and there is a barometer letting you know as well, so keep an eye out. Some of the questions you can be asked are "Name a superhero”. The key is you are trying to guess the most common answer, not just any old answer. If your answer is the most common, the very audience members who responded with your guess will back you up. You have to think of answers that the audience or survey participants might have chosen. Each round features a different category, and your goal is to type responses that align with the most common answers. Points are awarded based on how popular your answers are. The game typically includes multiple rounds, with the final rounds offering double or triple points. The person or team with the most points at the end wins. Creativity and speed are key since you have limited time to respond. The game allows for some flexibility with misspellings or variations, which can lead to unexpected wins. Whether you're playing solo or with friends, the challenge is to think quickly, predict what others might say, and sometimes laugh at how quirky or close-to-wrong answers can still score points.
