How to play Merge Restaurant

Your name is Mina, and you've got a new job. You’re in charge of a restaurant that is in a fantastic location, so it has a ton of potential, but it is in disarray as it was once shut down by a "strange man" and abandoned for some time. The only hitch is that this new restaurant is in worse shape than you thought, so you need to put in significant effort to clean it up. How do you clean up the restaurant? Merge Restaurant is an interactive merge game in which you combine objects to create new ones or to restore or clean them up. It’ll take some time to get this place back in shape and ready for customers, but that is your challenge. All the restaurant needs is your loving touch to breathe life back into it. You will need to wash tables, dust, replace furniture, and revamp the menu, all by merging items within the restaurant using your left mouse to click and drag. You merge to get what you need. Since the restaurant requires a lot of clean-up, you will get quite a few sponges to merge. Keep combining so you get even more useful items. The more you combine, the more unique and practical items will appear. Once you get your first restaurant in shape, you can move on to bigger and better restaurants and use your pro skills while you follow roller coaster love stories of the "strange man" who seemingly wants to shut your restaurant down.
