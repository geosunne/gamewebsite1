How to play Tile Guru

Tile Guru is one of those addictive games that you find yourself playing for hours, day after day. The aim of the game is fairly simple - you need to match sets of three tiles of the same suit. You'll start with a colorful array of fruit-themed tiles—grapes, watermelon, apples, cherries, and more. Click on each tile, and it will fall into the tile holder section. Once you get three matching tiles in the tile holder, they will disappear to make room for more. Early levels keep things simple, as all tiles are the same, making it easy to match a set of three. As the levels continue, the challenges get harder. Your tile holder is where the magic happens, and it is the part that requires the most strategy. The tile holder holds up to 7 tiles—if the holder fills up with unmatched tiles, the game is over! You will need to make sure you get as many similar tiles as possible, so be careful what you click on. As you progress, the challenge ramps up. Tiles become layered, so you’ll need to think strategically about which ones to free up first. If you grab too many unmatched tiles, you might run out of space before completing your sets. Plan ahead, keep an eye on your available space, and aim for those perfect matches to clear the board!
