How to play Food Truck Chef™: A Fun Cooking Game

Food Truck Chef is a fast-paced, fun, and addictive cooking game in which you manage your food truck and serve delicious dishes to customers on the go. There are plenty of places to choose from, all with similar gameplay styles. The pasta street food truck journey is a popular location to begin, and as you progress, you can unlock Pizza Street, Tex Mex Street, Sandwich Street, and more! The first thing you need to do is complete your training with Chef Sarah. You’ll learn the basics of the game, starting with your first customer. A customer orders pasta with red sauce. Plate the pasta, add the red sauce, place it on the counter, take the money, and voilà, your first order is complete! Your next customer wants a caffeine fix, so make sure to serve the coffee immediately after it’s filled so it’s nice and hot. After your training, you’re ready to run your pasta food truck! Keep cooking and serving to build up your experience and earn rewards. You earn coins and XP for completing orders, serving customers, and upgrading your kitchen. Fill the XP bar to level up and unlock rewards like coins and gems. You can watch ads to double your coins, which is crucial for buying new kitchen equipment and supplies. Use your coins to buy better kitchen equipment. New pans (600 coins), new plates (400 coins), and an espresso machine (600 coins) will help you serve customers faster. Remember to collect tips after serving customers. If you don't, new customers won’t show up! Get the hot plate as soon as possible to keep your sauces warm and serve dishes quickly. As you advance, you’ll unlock more additions to your dishes, like adding olives, cheese, and more to your pasta. Keep earning points, buying new equipment, and watching your food truck flourish with happy customers! New recipes will also be introduced. The white sauce, for example, burns easily if you don’t keep an eye on it, adding an extra challenge to your cooking! Game Tip To keep up with demand, have warm dishes prepped and ready to serve incoming guests. Always keep a close eye on your cooking times, especially for recipes that burn quickly.
