How to play GRWM Date Night

Ooh la la, it’s date night, and you’re about to get glam for a fabulous evening out! Let’s kick things off with your beauty routine. Start by perfecting your makeup: apply your foundation and blush, then move on to curling your lashes and adding a swipe of long-lasting mascara. Choose your lipstick: will you go natural or bold? It’s your night, your style! The better you look, the higher your "heart barometer" will rise, and once it’s high enough, you’ll be ready to move to the next round of prep. Now, onto the outfit and hairstyle! Choose from a sleek one-piece jumpsuit, a chic off-the-shoulder dress, or a sultry silk mini; there are endless options to experiment with. Complete your ensemble with accessories like jewelry, shoes, handbags, and more. How will you wear your hair? Straight, short, wavy? Try them all and find your perfect look! After your night out, it’s time to wind down with a skincare routine. Remove your makeup, cleanse, scrub, and apply serum and eye packs to leave your skin glowing. Finally, get cozy in your PJs: will it be comfy joggers or a chic silk short set? Add fun accessories like a headpiece, eye mask, phone, toothbrush, and some fuzzy slippers for the ultimate night-in vibe. Don’t forget to snap a photo of your stunning looks, download them, and share them with your friends!
