How to play Playground

You start playing Playground by adding characters to your game. Click on the heart icon to choose from a variety of characters, including male and female zombies, male and female humans, pigs, and even hybrid characters like zombie-pigs. Some special characters can only be unlocked through upgrade rewards. Next, click on the gun icon to access a wide range of weapons. You'll find everything from handguns and rifles to chainsaws, shovels, machine guns, and more. For heavier firepower, click on the grenade icon. Here you’ll find high-impact weapons like dynamite, grenades, rockets, and other powerful explosives that can help you survive tougher waves. Need to set up some defenses? Click on the cement block icon to browse materials for building protective walls. Options include bricks, grass, wood, asphalt, and other building elements. Click on the shirt icon to gear up. Equip helmets, protective vests, and other armor to boost your survival chances. The wooden crate icon opens up a collection of useful props like emergency cones, tables, chairs, computers, armoires, and more: perfect for customizing your environment or creating obstacles. Use the map icon to navigate the playground and plan your strategy. Gameplay Tip: When zombies touch humans, the humans turn into zombies, so position yourself wisely and keep your weapon ready. Your goal is to eliminate the threat and make your way across the playground as quickly and efficiently as possible.
