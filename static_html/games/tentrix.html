About TenTrix

TenTrix is a fantastic puzzle game set on a 10x10 playing board. The objective is to fit all the pieces together in rows like Tetris, but the pieces don’t fall.

How to play TenTrix

TenTrix provides you with a choice of 3 pieces at a time, and you must use all of those pieces before being able to see any more. As the game progresses, the free space will reduce as you struggle to find the ideal placement for each block. The game ends when you have a piece to place on the board but no space for it. At the end of each game, you receive a high score to aim for the next time you play. There is no time pressure, and you have the total freedom to use the available space on the board for your pieces. Tentrix is potentially endless, as in, if you have room for new pieces, it will continue indefinitely until you don't. Use the simple tap-and-drag mechanism to carry the pieces from the bottom of the screen onto the game board: good luck!

Controls

Drag the left mouse button to move a piece.

Tips for TenTrix

  • Don’t play TenTrix like it’s Tetris: you can build in any direction. Learn the different pieces so you know what to expect. Knowing what’s to come will allow you to plan ahead. Avoid creating too many small enclosures, as many pieces are large and could quickly end the game.
