How to play Squid Game Online

Choose your region and nickname to enter a game, then click join. You’ll be added to a room and be moments away from playing in this unforgiving challenge! In the first mini-game, you’ll have to sneak towards the doll without being seen in Red Light, Green Light.

Controls

Controls: WASD or arrow keys = move Space = jump LMB = scratch cookie / drag rope / hit E = pickup weapon G = drop weapon T = chat.
