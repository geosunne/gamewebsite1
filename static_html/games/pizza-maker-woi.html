How to play Pizza Maker

The gameplay in Pizza Maker is fairly simple. It is a one-button casual game where success is measured by how much fun you have playing. The game's objective is to get through several stages of cooking until your pizza is cooked and ready to serve. Shop for Ingredients You will need ingredients to make pizza, so the game begins by shopping for them. All necessary ingredients will be on a shelf. You must take the ingredients from the shelf and add them to your cart. Prepare the Ingredients In this step, you will slice tomatoes, pick basil, shred the cheese, and more. This is all the work involved in preparing the ingredients for making pizza. Make the Sauce This is where you unscrew the tomato jar and add it to a pan with water, oil, and salt. Shake the salt, stir, and pour all the ingredients into a bowl. Roll the Dough Mix flour, water, and salt. Lay the dough flat on the table and roll it out to form a pizza crust. Add Toppings Add all toppings onto your rolled pizza dough, including tomato, cheese, and whatever other ingredients you are working with. Bake the Pizza Place the pizza in a pizza oven and let it cook away. It will cook faster if you click your left mouse button fast enough.

Controls

Use the left mouse button to interact with the in-game UI as you follow the on-screen prompts and voice instructions.
