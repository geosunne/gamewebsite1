How to play Mr. Mine

The goal of the game is to mine for resources. The deeper you go, the greater the rewards. Mine deeper underground to obtain more resources and minerals to sell. You'll also encounter treasure chests the further you dig. These chests contain random rewards from small to vast amounts of money and resources. Unlike your typical idle game, Mister Mine requires active participation from players. This is evident in two ways: miners must find stones or minerals that require clicking, and players will encounter Bosses in the mines' depths that need to be fought back. You also need to monitor closely the percentage of capacity you reach. Once it’s at 100% your miners will stop working, so you’ll need to either sell all or some of your resources or use them to build or upgrade your tools. Your miners will be digging through 3 different worlds: Earth, up to 1032 km of depth Moon, from 1032 to 1132 km of depth Titan, after 1132 km of depth.
